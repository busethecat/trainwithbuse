# Privacy policy & terms — points for legal review

Not published (the `legal` folder is excluded in `.assetsignore`).
Pages: policy.html (EN), policy-de.html (DE), policy-tr.html (TR). The DE/TR texts are
faithful translations of the EN text; none of the points below were changed in them.

## Language and structure
1. **Which language applies.** EN has no clause; DE and TR both say German applies. Decide one rule for all three pages.
2. **No withdrawal notice.** German law requires a Widerrufsbelehrung and model form for online consumer contracts (coaching, programs, class memberships); none exists in any language. "All sales are generally final" for digital programs only holds if buyers explicitly consent and acknowledge losing the right.

## Privacy
3. **Consent by using the site.** "By using our website… you agree to the practices" isn't a valid legal basis under GDPR.
4. **Outdated service providers.** The list names Calendly, Payhip and Formspree. It omits Stripe, Supabase (stores PAR-Q health answers, contact messages, class reservations and class attendance), Resend, Cloudflare Workers, Google Fonts and jsDelivr. Zoom was added in November 2026 (see 18).
5. **Health data needs explicit consent.** Pregnancy status, limitations and PAR-Q answers are special-category data (GDPR Art. 9); no explicit consent is described.
6. **Cookies / browser storage.** Login session and preferences are kept in browser storage; check against TTDSG § 25.
7. **"Within 30 days".** GDPR says "without undue delay, at the latest within one month". Minor.

## Terms
8. **"Based in Berlin"** vs the Impressum address in Schöneiche bei Berlin.
9. **"Include applicable taxes (MwSt exempt)".** Translated as "no VAT charged under § 19 UStG". A small business isn't technically "exempt"; confirm the wording.
10. **Outdated payment providers.** Terms say "booked through Calendly" and "payment… through Payhip"; the site uses Stripe and its own booking form.
11. **Responsibility and liability.** "You accept full responsibility" and "total liability limited to the amount paid" are likely invalid in German standard terms for injury, intent or gross negligence (§ 309 Nr. 7 BGB).
12. **Berlin courts** as the only venue can't be imposed on consumers; choice of German law is limited for EU consumers.
13. **"Continued use constitutes acceptance"** of changed terms generally isn't valid against consumers.
14. **"Lifetime access".** Whose lifetime, and what happens if the service shuts down?

## Cancellation
15. **Full fee for late cancellation or no-show** is a flat-rate charge; standard terms must let the customer prove a lower loss (§ 309 Nr. 5 BGB).
16. **The 14-day refund rule.** "Reviewed individually" contradicts "full refund if under 20%", and overlaps with the statutory withdrawal right (2).
17. **Replay promise.** Group classes promise a 24-hour recording; make sure it is actually provided for every class.

## Added November 2026
18. **Zoom (live classes and 1:1 video sessions).** Listed as a service provider since November 2026. Zoom Video Communications, Inc. is a US company: check the data processing agreement (DPA) with Zoom, the legal basis for the transfer to the USA (EU–US Data Privacy Framework certification or Standard Contractual Clauses), and whether recordings of live classes (24-hour replay) need their own notice or consent, since participants may be visible or audible in them.
19. **Class attendance tracking.** The dashboard records which live classes a member joined and when (used for the membership and for progress badges). Check the stated purpose and legal basis (contract performance vs legitimate interest) and the retention period (currently: until the account is deleted).
20. **Class reservation form.** Name, email and class interests are stored for the launch announcement. Check the retention period once classes have launched.
