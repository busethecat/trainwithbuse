Real Personalisation

A personalised plan is only valuable
when someone understands you.

Most online fitness plans begin with a template. Your name is added, a few exercises are changed and the programme is called "personalised." This is different.

1:1 Online Coaching begins with you: your movement history, training experience, goals, schedule, available equipment, preferences and the obstacles that have made consistency difficult in the past.

Your programme is not created for an imaginary perfect week. It is designed for the life you actually live.

What We Can Work On

One goal, or several.

  • Strength and muscle development
  • Body composition
  • Mobility and movement quality
  • Core function and stability
  • Exercise technique
  • Returning to structured training
  • Building an effective home programme
  • Training around a demanding schedule
  • Improving confidence in the gym
  • Creating a sustainable weekly routine

When a concern falls outside my professional scope, I will tell you clearly and recommend the appropriate medical or therapeutic support. Good coaching includes knowing when not to guess.

Who Is This For?

This is ideal if you…

  • Want more individual attention than a group programme can provide.
  • Feel unsure whether you are performing exercises correctly.
  • Have specific goals that require a tailored approach.
  • Travel frequently or cannot commit to one physical location.
  • Want to train at home but need structure and progression.
  • Have tried generic programmes without lasting results.
  • Need accountability without aggressive fitness culture.
  • Want to learn how to train — not become dependent on a coach forever.

This is a collaborative process. I bring the scientific knowledge, programming and coaching experience. You bring honest feedback, participation and consistency. We build the plan together.

What Makes This Different

Not just access
to my time.

You are accessing a decade of coaching experience, academic training in movement and exercise science, and the ability to translate complex information into decisions you can actually use.

I do not coach by forcing every client into the same method. I observe, question, adjust and teach.

The goal is not only to make you work hard during one session. It is to help you understand your body well enough to make better training decisions long after the call ends.

Your location shouldn't decide
the quality of coaching you receive.

You may be training in your living room, a hotel gym or a fully equipped fitness facility. The environment changes. The standard of coaching does not. Bring your body, your questions and your goal — we'll build the rest from there.
