More Than a Workout

This is not simply
a workout with a trainer.

A private session should offer more than exercise selection and motivation. It should reveal how you move, why certain patterns keep returning, what your body currently needs and how your training should evolve over time.

The Buse Method is my most personal coaching format. Every session combines the precision of movement science with the human attention that cannot be replicated by an app, a template or a crowded fitness class.

I observe the details: how you breathe, stabilise, distribute load, respond to cues and compensate when a movement becomes difficult. Then I use that information to coach the person in front of me — not the programme on the page.

Why It's Different

Why in-person coaching
is different.

Online coaching can be highly effective. In-person coaching allows for another level of detail.

I can observe your movement from multiple angles, adjust your position immediately and use hands-on tactile feedback — with your consent — to help you understand a movement more clearly. Small changes in alignment, breathing, tempo or load distribution can completely change how an exercise feels and which tissues perform the work.

This immediate feedback is especially valuable when:

  • You are learning complex movement patterns
  • You struggle to feel the intended muscles
  • You compensate without noticing
  • You want to train confidently with heavier loads
  • You are returning after a long period away from exercise
  • You need a high level of attention and technical precision
  • Generic training has stopped producing meaningful progress

You are not corrected to make the movement look perfect.
You are coached to make it work better for your body.

Limited Availability

Why access
is limited.

The Buse Method is intentionally offered to a limited number of clients. This is not artificial exclusivity.

High-quality personal coaching requires preparation, observation, follow-up and mental presence. Limiting availability allows me to maintain that standard for every person I work with.

The purpose of the application is not to judge whether you are "good enough."
It is to determine whether we are the right professional match.

Is This For You?

This may be for you if…

  • You value expertise and individual attention over fitness trends.
  • You want to understand your body at a deeper level.
  • You are ready to commit to a structured process.
  • You want technical coaching that goes beyond verbal encouragement.
  • You appreciate honest feedback and thoughtful progression.
  • You want your training to support your life rather than dominate it.
  • You are looking for a long-term method, not a quick fix.

This may not be the right service if you are looking only for someone to count repetitions, provide a different workout every week or promise dramatic results without a meaningful process.
